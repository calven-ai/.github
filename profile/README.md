## Build on Calven

Every repo here is open source under the MIT license.

### Connect your AI tools

**[calven-mcp](https://github.com/calven-ai/calven-mcp)**: a remote MCP server that puts your approved GTM context, with sources, in Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, Cursor and Copilot. Sign in with OAuth. Its plugin adds skills for battlecards, deal prep, on-message drafts, customer evidence and win/loss dashboards.

### Run your marketing as code

**[marketing-as-code](https://github.com/calven-ai/marketing-as-code)**: a template repo for product marketing. Strategy, messaging, briefs and decisions live as plain text that agents read and write, and a person approves every change. Its product marketing skills also run in the Claude app, no repo needed.

### Demo on data that looks real

**[demoverse](https://github.com/calven-ai/demoverse)**: synthetic B2B sales data. It grows a fictional company's sales history in Salesforce, HubSpot, Google Drive and Slack, one week at a time: deals, call transcripts, emails and win/loss interviews, written by your coding agent. Calven built it to demo its own product.

### Migrate from Klue or Crayon

**[calven-migrate-from-klue](https://github.com/calven-ai/calven-migrate-from-klue)**: exports the cards and battlecards your Klue account can see to Markdown and JSON, in one command.

**[calven-migrate-from-crayon](https://github.com/calven-ai/calven-migrate-from-crayon)**: exports Crayon's answers on every competitor (battlecard, objections, proof points, win/loss stories) to Markdown and JSON, with citations.

Both are free command-line tools. You sign in with your own account through the vendor's official MCP server, the files stay on your machine, and nothing goes to Calven.
